How It Works

Analysis Methodology

1. Discover

10 persona agents analyze the codebase in parallel, each finding 8-15 friction points from their unique perspective by reading actual source code.

80-150 raw findings

2. Verify

All CRITICAL and HIGH severity findings are adversarially verified by independent skeptic agents who challenge assumptions and check for false positives.

adversarial skeptics

3. Synthesize

Results are deduplicated, cross-referenced across personas, severity-ranked, and organized into a prioritized remediation plan with effort estimates.

prioritized plan
